## Is my use commercial?

The license text is the final word. In short:

| Who / what | Commercial? |
|---|---|
| A company's team uses SuDuo, even only internally | **Yes**: register |
| A freelancer or consultant uses SuDuo for paid client work | **Yes**: register |
| A consultancy deploys SuDuo for a client company | **Yes**, for both organizations: one email can register both |
| You use SuDuo at home for a hobby project, personal study, or research for the benefit of public knowledge, with no anticipated commercial application | No |
| A school, university or other educational institution, or a public research organization | No |
| A charity, public safety or health organization, environmental protection organization, or government institution | No |

The noncommercial cases follow the **Personal Uses** and **Noncommercial Organizations** sections of the license. Use by a company or other for-profit organization is generally commercial use, unless the organization is one of those listed under **Noncommercial Organizations** (for example, an educational institution).

## Start now, register within 30 days

*This section is an additional permission from the licensor, on top of the licenses in [LICENSE](LICENSE).*

The licensor grants each company or other organization whose use is commercial (each legal entity separately) permission to use the software, including changing it and running changed versions, for any purpose within that organization and to provide services to its clients, during a **registration period of 30 consecutive calendar days** that starts on the day that organization first uses the software. This permission does not cover selling or otherwise distributing copies of the software, changed or not, outside the organization. Apart from that, the terms of [LICENSE](LICENSE) apply.

- Each organization gets **one** registration period, however many people, teams or installations start using the software.
- If the organization sends its registration (an email with the information listed under [How to register](#how-to-register)) within the registration period, this permission continues until the licensor issues a commercial license or declines the registration in writing. If the licensor declines, this permission ends 30 days after the written notice.
- Otherwise, once the registration period has ended, commercial use requires a commercial license from the licensor.

SuDuo never checks, counts or blocks anything. The software works the same with or without registration. Registration is a matter of the license, not of the code.

## How to register

Send an email to **license@suduo.dev** with the subject `SuDuo commercial registration` and include:

1. Organization legal name and country / region (list each legal entity that will use SuDuo)
2. Contact name and email
3. Estimated number of people who will use SuDuo
4. How you deploy it (for example: one cloud server on your own infrastructure, about 20 clients)
5. What you use it for, in a sentence or two

## What you receive

A written commercial license (PDF) that names your organizations and states the scope and term.
